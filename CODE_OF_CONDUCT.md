# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, harassment-free project for contributors of every
background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable
technical feedback, and do not publish another person's private information.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption,
and repeated bad-faith behavior are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the repository host. Maintainers must handle reports
confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
